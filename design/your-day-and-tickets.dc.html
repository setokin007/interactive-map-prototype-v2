Lāʻie, on Oʻahu's North Shore

Six islands. One day you won't forget.

Spend the afternoon with the people of Hawaiʻi, Samoa, Aotearoa, Fiji, Tahiti and Tonga, in villages where their ways of life are shared by the people who live them. Then stay for dinner and HĀ: Breath of Life. Here's how the day unfolds, and which ticket fits it.

Riding the coach? Pickup at Waikīkī hotels at 9:45 AM, arriving at the Center at 12:00 PM, just before the villages open at 12:15. The coach leaves the Center at 9:00 PM, after HĀ, and arrives in Waikīkī at 10:30 PM.

Driving? Parking is free at the Hukilau Marketplace, at the front gate. Come early for lunch at Pounders, the food trucks and the local shops.

12:15 – 5:30 PM

The six island villages

Each village shares its way of life in a 20-minute presentation, repeated through the afternoon. Wander between them at your own pace.

Plan on about 5 hours to see all six
1:00 PM daily

Huki on the lagoon

Every island arrives by canoe, one after another, with its own music and dance. The quickest way to meet all of Polynesia at once.

About 30 minutes with the pre-show
4:30 – 7:00 PM

Dinner: lūʻau or buffet

The Aliʻi Lūʻau honors Queen Liliʻuokalani with the Royal Court and the imu. The Gateway Buffet is relaxed, with live music.

Included in dinner packages
7:30 PM nightly

HĀ: Breath of Life

Over 100 performers tell one life, from birth to legacy, in the songs and dances of the islands, ending in fire.

About 90 minutes · reserved seating
The islands of Polynesia

Each village is hosted by people from that island, many of them students at BYU–Hawaiʻi next door. You'll be greeted in their language and invited to join in.
